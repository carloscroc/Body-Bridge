## Content Requirements by Exercise Type

### Compound Exercises (Primary Strength Movements)

**For compound movements (squats, deadlifts, presses, rows):**
- **Videos:** Maximum 2 minutes, multiple angles
  - Front view: Stance, grip, setup
  - Side view: Knee alignment, hip hinge depth, back position
  - Close-up: Full range of motion, lockout point
- **Images:** 800px, gym setting or outdoor fitness location
  - Equipment visible: Barbell, plates, rack, bench
  - Model: Multiple angles or split-screen if needed
- **Audio:** Tempo cues (3-1-3, 2-0-2), common mistakes, safety tips
- **Key Focus:** Multi-muscle activation, full ROM, controlled tempo

### Isolation Exercises (Targeted Muscle Groups)

**For isolation movements (curls, flys, extensions):**
- **Videos:** 1-2 minutes, single focus point
  - Front view: Isolate target muscle, show full range of motion
  - Close-up: Peak contraction, slow return
- **Images:** 800px, solid color background, equipment isolated
  - Model: Seated or positioned to emphasize isolation
  - Equipment visible: Single dumbbell or machine, correct setup
  - **Audio:** Squeeze cues, feel the muscle, avoid momentum
- **Key Focus:** Mind-muscle connection, constant tension, no cheating

### Core & Stability Exercises

**For plank, twists, rollouts:**
- **Videos:** 1 minute, static holds with transitions
  - Front view: Body alignment, neutral spine, engaged core
  - Side view: Form check, breathing cues
- **Images:** 800px, clear demonstration of body position
  - Model: Front/side angles, progression levels shown
  - Equipment visible: Minimal or no equipment, correct positioning
  - **Audio:** Breathing patterns, core engagement cues, progression suggestions
- **Key Focus:** Stability, proper form, gradual progression

### Cardio & HIIT Exercises

**For mountain climbers, burpees, battle ropes:**
- **Videos:** 1-2 minutes, continuous movement patterns
  - Front view: Full body coordination, proper landing mechanics
  - Side view: Hip drive, arm swing, foot placement
- **Images:** 800px, action shot, gym setting or fitness background
  - Model: Athletic clothing, proper footwear, appropriate setting
  - Equipment visible: No equipment or minimal props, correct form
  - **Audio:** Rep count suggestions, intensity cues, rest periods
- **Key Focus:** Power output, continuous movement, proper form

## Content Creation Guidelines

### Video Production Standards

**Format Specifications:**
- **Resolution:** 1080p minimum, 16:9 aspect ratio
- **Duration:** Maximum 2 minutes per video (1:00-2:00)
- **Codec:** H.264 for YouTube, AAC/Opus for Vimeo
- **Frame Rate:** 30fps for smooth motion
- **Bitrate:** 8-10 Mbps (YouTube recommended)

**Content Structure:**
1. **Intro (5-10 seconds):** Logo, title, exercise name
2. **Setup Demonstration (15-30 seconds):** Equipment, positioning, safety
3. **Movement Execution (30-90 seconds):** Primary movement with form cues
4. **Form Cues (15-30 seconds):** Tempo, common mistakes, safety tips
5. **Outro (5-10 seconds):** Summary, benefits, call-to-action

**Audio Requirements:**
- **Voiceover:** Clear, professional tone, moderate pace
- **Music:** Optional, low-volume background music
- **Sound Effects:** Clear cues for rep counts, transitions

### Image Production Standards

**Format Specifications:**
- **Resolution:** 800px minimum width, 400px minimum height
- **Aspect Ratio:** 4:3 for most exercises (vertical), 1:1 for full body shots
- **File Format:** JPG or PNG, high quality
- **Color Space:** sRGB for accurate color reproduction

**Photography Guidelines:**
1. **Lighting:** Professional studio lighting or natural outdoor light
   - Avoid harsh shadows on subjects
   - Ensure equipment is clearly visible
   - Use directional lighting to highlight muscle groups

2. **Subject Positioning:**
   - **Front View:** Show exercise setup, equipment, and starting position
   - **Side View:** Show movement mechanics, muscle activation
   - **Close-Up:** Demonstrate peak contraction or exercise completion
   - **Angles:** 2-3 angles per exercise for comprehensive coverage

3. **Model Requirements:**
   - **Athlete Type:** Matches target audience (beginner, intermediate, advanced)
   - **Form:** Correct exercise technique, no compensating movements
   - **Attire:** Professional fitness attire, appropriate for exercise type
   - **Equipment:** Clean, properly configured, correct for exercise

4. **Background Considerations:**
   - **Solid Colors:** Plain wall, neutral background, or blurred gym environment
   - **Environment:** Clean, distraction-free setting
   - **Depth of Field:** Shallow to keep focus on athlete

### Quality Assurance Checklist

**Video QA:**
- [ ] Form demonstration is clear and accurate
- [ ] Audio instructions are audible and understandable
- [ ] Tempo cues match exercise type
- [ ] Video length within 2-minute limit
- [ ] Multiple angles shown when needed
- [ ] Intro/outro consistent across all videos
- [ ] Branding elements (logo, colors) are consistent

**Image QA:**
- [ ] Exercise accurately represents the movement
- [ ] Equipment is clearly visible and correct
- [ ] Athlete demonstrates proper form
- [ ] Lighting highlights the subject effectively
- [ ] Resolution meets 800px minimum standard
- [ ] Professional photography quality

**Content Validation:**
- [ ] Video content matches exercise requirements
- [ ] Image content accurately reflects exercise
- [ ] All content follows established guidelines
- [ ] URLs are functional and accessible
- [ ] Branding is consistent across all materials
